# Attribution Methods

Choose the smallest method that answers the question. These are options, not a
mandatory sequence.

- Change attribution: compare a baseline period with a comparison period and
  rank absolute deltas and contribution shares.
- Dimension attribution: compare regions, channels, customers, products, or
  other business dimensions.
- Metric-tree decomposition: explain a formula such as revenue = orders x
  average order value, or profit = revenue - cost.
- Anomaly attribution: identify unusual dates or segments, then drill into the
  dimensions that explain the outlier.
- Funnel attribution: locate the stage where volume or conversion is lost.
- Observational attribution: describe co-movement when causal evidence is not
  available.

Prefer absolute contribution alongside percentage change. A small segment with
a large growth rate may not explain much of the total movement. Compare periods
with compatible duration, currency, metric caliber, and data coverage.

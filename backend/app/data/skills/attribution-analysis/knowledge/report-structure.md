# Attribution Report Structure

Compose only sections supported by the evidence. A typical report may include:

- `executive_summary`: the headline and the most important findings.
- `metric_change`: baseline, comparison, delta, and delta percentage.
- `driver_ranking`: ordered positive and negative contributors.
- `time_pattern`: trend or anomaly chart.
- `dimension_breakdown`: supporting breakdown tables.
- `hypotheses`: plausible explanations that are not directly proven.
- `recommendations`: actions grounded in the findings.
- `quality_notes`: coverage, truncation, caliber, currency, and data caveats.
- `limitations`: explicit limits such as observational evidence or small samples.

Do not create empty sections. A short report is preferable when only one method
or one reliable driver is available.
